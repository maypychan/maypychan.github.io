May Pik Yu Chan

PhD in linguistics

Hello! I am a PhD in Linguistics from the University of Pennsylvania (May 2026). My main research interests are in phonetics and phonology. The broad question driving my work is how patterns of vocal variation inform our understanding of linguistic structure. My dissertation explores this through vowel-pitch interactions across contexts ranging from everyday speech to singing, integrating acoustic, articulatory, and perceptual perspectives. I'm also interested in the consequences of variation for speech technology. I am affiliated with the Penn Phonetics Laboratory and the Language Variation and Cognition Lab. I was a MindCORE Graduate Student affiliate. I also served as the Student Council Representative for the Musical Acoustics Technical Committee for the Acoustical Society of America.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher in the The Voice Lab in the Department of Communication Sciences and Disorders at Syracuse University, where I explore mechanisms of vocal‑effort adaptation in immersive virtual‑reality communication environments.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I completed my B.A. at the University of Hong Kong, double majoring in Linguistics and Music, with a minor in German. At HKU, I was affiliated with the Language Development Laboratory.

You may find my CV here, and my Penn Grad Talks presentation here. You’re welcome to visit my google drive, and GitHub as well.
